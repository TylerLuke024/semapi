# Different Kinds of Data Centers
- Enterprise Data Centers – Owned and operated by a company to store and manage its own data and applications.
- Colocation Data Centers – A facility where multiple companies rent space, power, cooling, and network connections for their servers.
- Cloud Data Centers – Run by providers like AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud, offering computing resources over the internet.
- Edge Data Centers – Smaller facilities located close to users or devices to reduce latency and process data faster.
- Hyperscale Data Centers – Extremely large facilities designed to support massive amounts of computing and storage, typically operated by major cloud and technology companies.
- Managed Data Centers – A third party manages the hardware, networking, security, and maintenance for a customer.
